# Public Pages

## Direction

Public pages should present ServiceBridge as a digital service mediation and payment platform. They should build trust, explain service flow, and guide users into service and variant selection.

## Rules

- The first viewport must clearly communicate ServiceBridge and the service request flow.
- Service discovery should emphasize service, variant, required fields, price, and fulfillment expectations.
- Avoid shopping-cart-heavy language and retail product framing.
- Public pages should use real service context or clearly marked placeholders when real content is not yet available.
- Primary actions should lead toward service selection or sign-in, not generic marketing capture.
- Payment, credential, and fulfillment trust cues should be visible without being alarmist.

## Sections

- Hero.
- Service categories.
- How fulfillment works.
- Trust and payment guidance.
- Frequently asked questions.
- Footer.
