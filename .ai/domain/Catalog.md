# Catalog

## Product Model

ServiceBridge catalog entries represent digital services and variants, not retail merchandise.

## Rules

- Users select a service and variant before checkout.
- Variant selection drives pricing and required order fields.
- Catalog UI should communicate fulfillment requirements clearly.

## Guidance

- Avoid generic e-commerce assumptions.
- Keep service and variant language consistent.
- Do not couple catalog display models directly to EF entities.
